A STRAIGHTFORWARD COMPARISON

Shallowtail vs Razorpay Payment Links

Both send a UPI payment link over WhatsApp. The difference is what you have to do before the first one goes out — and what you pay after.

Quick verdict

Choose Shallowtail if…

You're a solo seller taking occasional payments and you don't want an account, KYC documents, or a percentage of every sale. Everything lives in your browser; you can have a working link in under a minute.

Choose Razorpay if…

You sell at volume, need GST invoices, automated refund workflows, or must prove a specific payment landed without checking your own bank statement.

Where Razorpay genuinely wins

Be honest here — this is what makes the page rank and what stops buyers feeling tricked.

  • Order-level tracking. Razorpay assigns a unique payment ID to every transaction, giving a clean order trail a personal UPI handle (or a local list) cannot produce.[1]
  • Automatic confirmation. No screenshot-chasing; the platform tells you the money arrived.
  • Refunds and disputes have an audit trail instead of being manual reverse transfers.[1]

Where Shallowtail wins

  • Zero friction to first payment. No PAN, no GSTIN, no "your account is under review."
  • No fees, ever. There's no company behind it taking a cut.
  • Privacy by architecture. Merchant details stay in your browser and are encoded into each link rather than stored in a database.
  • Free and open source. Fork it, self-host it, read exactly what it does with your data.

The honest tradeoff

Shallowtail gives you a link and a QR code. It does not verify that any payment cleared — you confirm that in your own UPI app. If your business needs that verification automatically, that's a real product and it costs money to run. For a home baker, a tutor, or a thrift seller, it usually isn't worth the paperwork.

[1] Razorpay's Payment Links documentation and pricing information. Fees and product features can vary by payment method and account.
